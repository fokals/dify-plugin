# Privacy

This plugin sends your Fokals key and the inputs you give its tools (company names and website addresses, filters such as a technology, a buying topic or a country, and dates) to the Fokals API at `https://api.fokals.com/v1` over HTTPS, and returns the answers to Dify. It sends nothing to any other service.

The plugin itself does not collect, store or log any user data. It keeps no copy of your key, your inputs or the answers.

Fokals keeps a record of each call against your account (which tool, when, and which companies were returned) so it can show your usage and keep your balance. Fokals holds company data only, never data about people. Fokals does not share your inputs with third parties.

Fokals processes this data under its privacy policy: https://www.fokals.com/privacy
